1. Set up your ICP (feeds all AI scoring)

Skout uses your Ideal Customer Profile to score prospects and personalize AI suggestions.

#What you doWhat you should seePass?
1.1 In the sidebar, go to Discover → ICP
(or Help → Setup wizard)		 ICP form with industry, company size, geography ☐
1.2 Fill in your target industries and company size, then Save Success message; settings stay after refresh ☐
1.3 Go to Discover → Prospect search, search for a job title (e.g. "VP Sales") Results list or a clear "no results" message ☐
1.4 Click one prospect to open their detail panel Name, company, and any ICP / score info shown ☐

2. Dexter AI chat (floating assistant)

This is the main AI command bar — available while you work anywhere in the app.

#What you doWhat you should seePass?
2.1 Click the round AI button at the bottom-right of the screen Chat panel opens with a text box and suggestion chips ☐
2.2 Type: "Take me to sequences" and press Enter App navigates to Sequences, or Dexter gives a clear link ☐
2.3 Open Dexter again. Ask: "How healthy is deliverability?" A written answer (not blank, not a server error) ☐
2.4 Ask: "Open AI Review" Navigates to the AI Review page ☐
2.5 Close the chat, go to CRM → Contacts, open Dexter again Chat still works from the new page ☐
2.6 Optional — voice: tap the microphone icon, say a short question, stop Your words appear as text; Dexter replies ☐

2A. Create a sequence through Dexter (with safety check)

#What you doWhat you should seePass?
2A.1 In Dexter, ask: "Create an outbound sequence called Test Cadence with one email step" A yellow "Review before executing" box with scope and side effects ☐
2A.2 Click Confirm and run Success message; new sequence appears under Outreach → Sequences ☐

Note: Step 2A needs the latest app version. If you only get text with no review box, tell engineering to deploy the newest frontend.

3. AI Review — approve AI-written emails before sending

#What you doWhat you should seePass?
3.1 Sidebar: Intelligence → AI Review Queue of drafts, or "nothing to review" empty state ☐
3.2 Go to Discover → Prospect search, open a prospect, find Generate draft (or similar) Button to create a personalized email draft ☐
3.3 Click generate, then open AI Review again New draft listed as Needs review ☐
3.4 Open the draft, read subject and body, click Approve or Reject Status updates; draft moves out of "Needs review" ☐
3.5 Sidebar: Outreach → Draft auto-approve (settings) Threshold sliders / settings page loads ☐

4. Inbox — AI suggest reply

#What you doWhat you should seePass?
4.1 Sidebar: Outreach → Inbox → Conversations Thread list (or empty state if no mail connected) ☐
4.2 Select any conversation with at least one message Message thread opens on the right ☐
4.3 Click Suggest reply AI draft text appears in the reply area (may take a few seconds) ☐
4.4 Edit the draft if you want, then send or discard Normal send flow works, or clear error if mailbox not set up ☐

5. CRM 360 — AI next best action

AI recommends what to do next for a specific contact or deal, based on real CRM history.

#What you doWhat you should seePass?
5.1 Sidebar: CRM → Contacts — pick a contact that has some activity Contact detail page ☐
5.2 Sidebar: CRM → Account 360 360 view with company / person toggle ☐
5.3 Load your contact (paste ID or search if the page asks) Deals, tasks, signals sections (some may be empty) ☐
5.4 Find the Suggest next action card and click it Headline + short rationale (e.g. "Schedule a call because…") ☐
5.5 Click Create task on the suggestion Confirmation that a task was created ☐
5.6 Optional: choose Enroll in sequence if shown Enrollment succeeds or clear message why not ☐

6. Decisions — act on AI recommendations

#What you doWhat you should seePass?
6.1 Sidebar: Intelligence → Decision views Open decisions list ☐
6.2 If there is a Create from next best action option, use a contact ID from step 5 New item appears in the queue ☐
6.3 Open a decision, pick an option or dismiss Item leaves the open queue ☐

7. Dexter Orchestrator — governed AI plans

For GTM actions that need human approval before they run.

#What you doWhat you should seePass?
7.1 Sidebar: Outreach → Dexter Orchestrator Command center with summary cards and plan form ☐
7.2 In the plan brief box, type a goal (e.g. "Enroll high-fit SaaS VPs in a cadence") and click Propose Plan ID shown; item appears under pending approvals ☐
7.3 Click Approve Success message ☐
7.4 Click Invoke (only works after approve) Invoke succeeds ☐
7.5 Click Record learning Outcome saved on the plan ☐
7.6 Scroll to Recent policy blocks List of blocks or "no recent denials" (no crash) ☐

8. Policy Gateway — control how autonomous AI is

#What you doWhat you should seePass?
8.1 Sidebar: Automation → Policy Gateway Table of actions with modes: ask, auto, draft, approve ☐
8.2 Change one action (e.g. plan invoke) to approve and save Setting persists after page refresh ☐
8.3 Change it back to your preferred mode Saves successfully ☐

9. LinkedIn Voice — AI script for voice notes

#What you doWhat you should seePass?
9.1 Sidebar: Outreach → LinkedIn Voice Voice wizard (steps: pick contact → draft → review → handoff) ☐
9.2 If prompted, connect LinkedIn under Deliverability first Connected account shown ☐
9.3 Pick an eligible 1st-degree contact and generate a script AI-written script text appears ☐
9.4 Optional: play audio preview Audio plays (preview only — not sent to LinkedIn) ☐
9.5 Use mobile handoff (QR code), send manually in LinkedIn app, then confirm in Skout Activity logged on contact timeline ☐

10. CRO Copilot — executive AI (admin only)

#What you doWhat you should seePass?
10.1 Sidebar: Intelligence → CRO Copilot Pipeline summary cards + chat panel ☐
10.2 Ask: "Which reps are behind on pipeline this week?" Answer based on your workspace data ☐
10.3 If you have a non-admin test account: try opening CRO Copilot Access denied message (not a crash) ☐

11. Enrichment & signals (AI context)

  5. Account 360 → Suggest next action (5.4)
  6. Dexter Orchestrator → Propose → Approve → Invoke (7.2–7.4)
  7. CRO Copilot → one pipeline question (10.2)
